# E-commerce Sales Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data to identify sales trends, top-performing products, and valuable customers.

## Business Task

The company wants to analyze sales trends and identify its most valuable products and customers.

## Tools

* Microsoft Excel
* Tableau

## Key Insights

* Sales were relatively stable from January to March.
* Smartphone had the highest total sales at $17,519.83.
* CUST1558 had the highest total spending at $2,404.44.
* New customers generated the highest total sales at $27,003.29.

## Dashboard

The Tableau dashboard includes:

* Monthly Sales
* Sales by Product
* Top 5 Customers
* Sales by Customer Segment
